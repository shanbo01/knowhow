# Design-partner pilot agreement — working draft

Status: commercial/legal outline only. Qatar-focused counsel must produce and approve the executable agreement. No pilot invitation may be issued for real customer data until both parties sign the final agreement, DPA, acceptable-use policy, privacy notice acknowledgement, and subprocessor schedule.

## 1. Parties and order form

- Provider: `[incorporated legal name, registration, Qatar address]`.
- Design partner: `[legal name, registration, address]`.
- Contacts: commercial, privacy, security, billing, and incident contacts for each party.
- Pilot: named department, approved use case, start/end dates, up to 30 days, up to 100 invited users, configured storage/creator limits, and agreed workspaces.
- Fees: free pilot unless the signed order states otherwise; no card, public checkout, automatic paid conversion, or live payment processing.

## 2. Access and use

Access is invitation-only and nontransferable. The design partner controls its users, workspace roles, audiences, content, and lawful instructions. Users must use current supported Chrome/Edge versions, protect authentication/recovery credentials, enable administrator MFA, and report suspected compromise.

Use is limited to ordinary internal business-process information approved for the pilot. Credentials, secrets, payment/card data, health data, national IDs, government-classified data, children's data, and sensitive/special-category data are prohibited. The design partner must not circumvent limits, probe other tenants, introduce malware, scrape the service, resell access, or use the pilot for unlawful or high-risk decisions.

## 3. Service and support

KnowHow provides the hosted application, private redacted-media storage, browser extension, operational notifications, in-app support, export, and lifecycle controls described in the approved documentation. Initial support response target is one business day in Qatar business hours. The parties acknowledge that this is a design-partner pilot, not enterprise GA.

Internal operational targets are database RPO up to 24 hours and best-effort RTO within one business day. They are not service levels, credits, warranties, or guarantees. Independent media disaster recovery is not included.

## 4. Changes and feedback

KnowHow may update or roll back the service/extension for security, reliability, and pilot learning, with reasonable notice for material changes when practicable. Feedback may be used to improve KnowHow without identifying the design partner or disclosing its confidential information. `[Counsel to define any licence and publicity/reference rights; default is no public logo/name use without written approval.]`

## 5. Data protection and security

The approved DPA, privacy notice, subprocessor list, retention schedule, security overview, and incident terms are incorporated. The design partner is responsible for lawful collection/instructions and user notices; KnowHow processes customer content only to provide, secure, support, and comply with the service/contract. Exceptional support access is customer-approved, MFA-reauthenticated, reason-bound, short-lived, notified, and audited.

Each party must notify the other through the incident contacts of relevant security/privacy incidents according to the final counsel-approved timing and content. Neither party should include credentials or unnecessary customer content in incident communications.

## 6. Confidentiality and ownership

Each party protects the other's confidential information with at least reasonable care and uses it only for the pilot. Customer content remains the design partner's/customer licensors' material. KnowHow and its licensors retain the service, extension, documentation, aggregate content-free analytics, and improvements. The design partner grants only the rights necessary to operate the pilot.

Required exceptions, compelled disclosure, residuals, injunctive relief, and confidentiality duration must be finalized by counsel.

## 7. Term, suspension, export, and deletion

The pilot starts and ends on the signed dates unless extended or converted in writing. Expiry enters seven days of read-only grace (sign-in, viewing, export, and account/settings), followed by suspension. Notices continue through the retention period. At 90 days after original expiry, KnowHow creates a critical deletion-approval case; no content is purged without explicit approval, current TOTP, and typed confirmation.

Either party may terminate for material breach, security risk, prohibited data, insolvency, or convenience on `[reviewed notice]`. Immediate suspension is permitted to prevent harm, comply with law, or contain compromise. Export should be requested before deletion. The final terms must define post-termination return/deletion, backup limitations, legal hold, and receipt obligations.
